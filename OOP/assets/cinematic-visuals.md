# Визуальные материалы кинематографичной версии

Оба изображения созданы встроенным инструментом image_gen, проверены визуально и сохранены в проекте. Альфа-канал атласа рыцаря сохранён. HTML встраивает изображения как data URI и работает без сети.

- `cinematic-knight-walk.png` — восемь фаз походки одного рыцаря, сетка 4 × 2, прозрачный фон.
- `cinematic-environments.png` — четыре панорамы, сетка 2 × 2: врата, мост в лесу, библиотека и цитадель.

## Промпт: рыцарь

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: transparent animation source sprite atlas for a premium cinematic educational website. Generate ONE wide PNG containing exactly EIGHT separate full-body views of the SAME realistically proportioned medieval knight in a 4-column by 2-row evenly spaced grid, no borders, no background, truly transparent alpha. Each cell equal dimensions, each figure centered with full feet and plume inside cell, soles aligned to the same relative baseline. The knight faces RIGHT in strict side/three-quarter side profile, walking from left to right. Eight sequential walk cycle poses in reading order: contact, down, passing, up, opposite contact, down, passing, up. Adult human anatomy 7.5 heads tall, normal modest helmet size, broad articulated shoulders, long anatomical legs, fully enclosed late-medieval steel plate armour with beveled edges, polished worn silver and charcoal reflections, etched narrow gold trim, chainmail at joints, gauntlets, articulated cuisses/poleyns/greaves/sabatons, subdued dark burgundy linen cape flowing naturally to left. A long sword sheathed slantwise on left hip, LEFT hand resting near pommel. Small realistic kite shield on left forearm, no cross or religious symbol, subtle geometric engraving. Cinematic photorealistic costume/VFX asset, tactile steel, leather straps, micro scratches, directional rim lighting amber from upper left and cool blue fill. No chibi, no mascot, no cartoon, no oversized head or feet, no toy, no floating or cut-off limbs, no labels, no text, no grid lines. The same costume and scale across all 8 poses. Plenty transparent spacing between figures. High detail.
